Privacy Policy

SpendSense Pro · Last updated: January 1, 2024

Short version: SpendSense Pro stores your financial data locally on our server. We do not sell, share, or monetize your personal data. You own your data and can export or delete it at any time.

1. What Data We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number
  • Financial data: expenses, income, bills, investments, loans, savings goals (entered by you)
  • Usage data: pages visited, features used (for app improvement)
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type (for security and fraud prevention)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide the SpendSense Pro service
  • To generate financial insights and analytics for you
  • To send bill reminders and budget alerts (in-app notifications)
  • To process subscription payments (via Razorpay — see Payment Data below)
  • To improve the product and fix bugs

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal financial data with advertisers, data brokers, or third parties. We use the following third-party services:

  • Razorpay — for payment processing. Payment card/UPI data is handled by Razorpay and subject to their privacy policy.
  • Google OAuth — if you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us.

4. Payment Data

We never store full card numbers or CVVs. Payments are processed by Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant). We store only order IDs and payment status.

5. Data Security

  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt (12 rounds)
  • Sessions are HTTP-only cookies
  • API endpoints are rate-limited and protected with security headers
  • HTTPS enforced in production

6. Your Rights (DPDP / GDPR)

  • Access: Export all your data in JSON format from Settings → Export Data
  • Deletion: Permanently delete your account and all data from Settings → Delete Account
  • Correction: Update any inaccurate data from within the app
  • Portability: Download your data in CSV or JSON format

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all data is permanently deleted immediately.

8. Cookies

We use one session cookie for authentication. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Children's Privacy

SpendSense Pro is not intended for users under 13 years of age.

10. Contact

For privacy concerns or data requests, contact: privacy@spendsense.app
